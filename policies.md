# TechMart Customer Policies

## Shipping Policy

### Standard Shipping

Cost: $4.99

Estimated Delivery:
3–5 Business Days

### Express Shipping

Cost: $12.99

Estimated Delivery:
1–2 Business Days

### Free Shipping

Orders above $50 qualify for free standard shipping.

### Same-Day Dispatch

Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST are dispatched on the same business day.

### Shipping Coverage

Available throughout all 50 U.S. states.

### International Shipping

Currently unavailable.

---

## Return Policy

### Standard Products

Return window:
15 days from purchase date.

### Electronics

Return window:
7 days from purchase date.

### Return Conditions

Items must:

* Be unused
* Be in original packaging
* Include all accessories
* Include original manuals
* Include proof of purchase

### Non-Returnable Items

* Final Sale items
* Gift cards
* Digital downloads
* Software licenses

---

## Refund Policy

### Processing Time

Refunds are processed within 5 business days after inspection.

### Refund Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

### Non-Refundable Fees

Shipping charges are non-refundable unless:

* Wrong item received
* Item arrived damaged
* Item arrived defective

### Credit Card Refunds

Banks may require an additional 3–5 business days.

---

## Exchange Policy

### Eligibility

General Products:
30 Days

Electronics:
15 Days

### Conditions

Products must be returned in original condition.

### Price Difference

If replacement costs more:
Customer pays difference.

If replacement costs less:
Difference refunded within 5 business days.

---

## Warranty Policy

### Manufacturer Warranty

Coverage:
1 Year

Covered:

* Manufacturing defects
* Hardware failures
* Internal component defects

Not Covered:

* Water damage
* Physical damage
* Accidental damage
* Unauthorized repairs
* Product modifications

### Extended Warranty

Available for:

* Laptops
* Desktop Computers

Duration:
Additional 2 Years

---

## Order Cancellation Policy

Orders may be cancelled without penalty before shipment.

Orders already shipped cannot be cancelled and must follow the return process.

Refunds for cancelled orders are processed within 3–5 business days.

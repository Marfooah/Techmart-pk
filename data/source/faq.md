# Frequently Asked Questions — TechMart Pakistan

## Orders

Q: How can I track my order?

A: You can track your order from your account dashboard or using the tracking number sent via SMS and email. We partner with TCS, Leopards, PostEx, and M&P for deliveries across Pakistan.

---

Q: Can I cancel my order?

A: Yes, if your order has not yet been shipped. Contact support@techmart.pk or use the AI chat with your order number.

---

Q: Can I modify my shipping address?

A: Address changes are possible before your order is dispatched. Contact us as soon as possible with your order number.

---

## Shipping

Q: Do you offer free shipping?

A: Yes. Orders above PKR 5,000 qualify for free standard shipping within Pakistan.

---

Q: How long does shipping take?

A: Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days for major cities. Express shipping takes 1–2 business days. Remote areas may take 5–7 business days.

---

Q: Do you ship internationally?

A: No. We currently deliver within Pakistan only.

---

Q: Which courier do you use?

A: We use TCS, Leopards Courier, PostEx, and M&P depending on your city and product type.

---

## Returns & Refunds

Q: How do I return an item?

A: Contact support@techmart.pk or use the Returns section in your account with your order number and reason for return.

---

Q: How long do refunds take?

A: Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. Bank transfers may take an additional 3–5 business days.

---

Q: Are shipping charges refundable?

A: Shipping charges are refundable only when TechMart is at fault (wrong item, damaged, or defective product).

---

Q: Can I return on Cash on Delivery orders?

A: Yes. Refunds for COD orders are issued via bank transfer or JazzCash/EasyPaisa.

---

## Warranty

Q: Is my product covered under warranty?

A: Most electronics include a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Extended warranty is available for laptops and desktops.

---

Q: What does the warranty cover?

A: Manufacturing defects and hardware failures during normal use. Accidental damage is not covered.

---

Q: Does the warranty cover water or physical damage?

A: No. Warranty does not cover water damage, physical damage, or unauthorized repairs.

---

## Account

Q: Can I create an account?

A: Yes. Register with your email to view orders, track shipments, and chat with our AI support assistant.

---

Q: Can I delete my account?

A: Yes. Contact support@techmart.pk to request account deletion.

---

Q: How do I reset my password?

A: Use the "Forgot Password" option on the login page or contact support.

---

## Payments

Q: Is Cash on Delivery available?

A: Yes, COD is available for most products across Pakistan. A small COD fee may apply for orders under PKR 2,000.

---

Q: Do you accept JazzCash and EasyPaisa?

A: Yes. Both are accepted at checkout and for refunds.

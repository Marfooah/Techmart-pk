# TechMart Pakistan — Customer Policies

## Shipping Policy

### Standard Shipping

Cost: PKR 250  
Estimated Delivery: 3–5 business days (major cities)

### Express Shipping

Cost: PKR 500  
Estimated Delivery: 1–2 business days (major cities)

### Free Shipping

Orders above PKR 5,000 qualify for free standard shipping nationwide.

### Same-Day Dispatch

Orders placed before 2:00 PM PKT (Monday–Saturday) are dispatched the same business day from our Lahore warehouse.

### Shipping Coverage

Available across Pakistan including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, and other cities.

### Remote Areas

Remote or hard-to-reach areas may require 1–2 additional business days. Additional charges may apply.

### International Shipping

Currently unavailable. We deliver within Pakistan only.

### Courier Partners

TCS, Leopards Courier, PostEx, M&P Courier

---

## Return Policy

### Standard Products

Return window: 15 days from delivery date.

### Electronics

Return window: 7 days from delivery date.

Includes: laptops, smartphones, tablets, gaming consoles, monitors.

### Return Conditions

Items must:

* Be unused (electronics may be opened for inspection only)
* Be in original packaging with all accessories
* Include original manuals and warranty cards
* Include proof of purchase (order confirmation)

### Non-Returnable Items

* Final sale / clearance items
* Gift cards
* Digital downloads
* Software licenses (once activated)
* Personal care electronics (earbuds, trimmers) if opened

### Damaged or Defective Items

Report within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We will arrange pickup via our courier partner.

---

## Refund Policy

### Processing Time

Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after inspection at our Lahore facility.

### Refund Method

* Card payments: refunded to original card
* JazzCash/EasyPaisa: refunded to same wallet
* COD orders: refunded via bank transfer or mobile wallet
* Bank transfer: refunded to provided account

### Non-Refundable Fees

COD handling fee and express shipping charges are non-refundable unless TechMart is at fault.

### Shipping Charges Refundable When

* Wrong item received
* Item arrived damaged
* Item arrived defective
* Item not as described

---

## Exchange Policy

### Eligibility

General products: 15 days from delivery  
Electronics: 7 days from delivery

### Conditions

Products must be returned in original condition with all packaging.

### Price Difference

If replacement costs more: customer pays the difference in PKR.  
If replacement costs less: difference refunded within 5 business days.

---

## Warranty Policy

### Manufacturer Warranty

Coverage: 1 year from delivery date

Covered:

* Manufacturing defects
* Hardware failures
* Internal component defects

Not covered:

* Water damage
* Physical damage
* Accidental damage
* Unauthorized repairs
* Product modifications
* Damage from power surges (use a stabilizer)

### Extended Warranty

Available for laptops and desktop computers.  
Additional 2 years coverage.  
Contact support@techmart.pk for pricing.

---

## Order Cancellation Policy

Orders may be cancelled without penalty before shipment.

Orders already shipped cannot be cancelled — please use the return process.

Refunds for cancelled orders are processed within 3–5 business days.

---

## Cash on Delivery Policy

COD available for orders up to PKR 100,000.

Customer must inspect package before accepting delivery.

Refuse delivery if package appears damaged and contact support immediately.

---

## Privacy

Customer data is stored securely and used only for order fulfillment and support. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

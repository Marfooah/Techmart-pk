# Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders

Q: How can I track my order?

A: Customers can track orders through their account dashboard or shipment tracking email.

---

Q: Can I cancel my order?

A: Yes, if the order has not been shipped.

---

Q: Can I modify my shipping address?

A: Address changes are possible before shipment.

---

## Shipping

Q: Do you offer free shipping?

A: Yes. Orders above $50 qualify for free standard shipping.

---

Q: How long does shipping take?

A: Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express shipping takes 1–2 business days.

---

Q: Do you ship internationally?

A: No. Shipping is limited to the United States.

---

## Returns & Refunds

Q: How do I return an item?

A: Contact [support@techmart.com](mailto:support@techmart.com) with your order number.

---

Q: How long do refunds take?

A: Refunds are processed within 5 business days after inspection.

---

Q: Are shipping charges refundable?

A: Only when TechMart is responsible for the issue.

---

## Warranty

Q: Is my product covered under warranty?

A: Most electronics include a 1-year manufacturer warranty.

---

Q: What does the warranty cover?

A: Manufacturing defects and hardware failures during normal use.

---

Q: Does the warranty cover accidental damage?

A: No.

---

## Account

Q: Can I create an account?

A: Yes.

---

Q: Can I delete my account?

A: Yes. Contact support to request account deletion.

---

Q: How do I reset my password?

A: Use the "Forgot Password" option on the login page.
